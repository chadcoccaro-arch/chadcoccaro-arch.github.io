GTA 6 Shorts

GTA 6 Shorts is a private, single-user tool that helps its owner, Chad Coccaro, turn his own GTA 6 gameplay recordings into short vertical videos for his YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Reads new recordings from the owner's own Google Drive folder and makes short clips with captions.
  • Lists the clips in the owner's own Google Sheet so he can approve or reject each one.
  • Uploads approved clips to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Reads view and revenue statistics for that channel so the owner can see what performs well.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the owner signs in with Google, and it only uses the owner's own account.
